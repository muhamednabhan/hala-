# Dtec and workspaces at DSO

Tags: [PUBLISHED] official page, say it plainly. [REPORTED] secondary source, say "reportedly". [NOT PUBLISHED] say you do not know.

## What Dtec is
- [PUBLISHED] Dtec (Dubai Technology Entrepreneurship Campus) is part of DSO under DIEZ, described as the largest tech startup coworking space in the Middle East. It is about 108,000 square feet and has 1,000+ startups from 90 countries.
- [PUBLISHED] Locations: TechnoHub 1 (phone 04 501 5367) and Dubai Digital Park Building A5 (phone 04 501 5791). DSO's coworking page lists three campuses: the DSO headquarters building, Dtec TechnoHub 1 and Dtec A5 in Dubai Digital Park.
- [PUBLISHED] Dtec emails: new clients dtec.sales@dtec.ae, existing clients dtecsupport@dtec.ae.
- [PUBLISHED] Hours: members have access 24 hours a day, including weekends. The Dtec office and sales desk is open 7:30 AM to 3 PM.
- [PUBLISHED] Facilities: wifi, a cafe, games areas, free tea and coffee, lockers, printing and corporate labs.
- [PUBLISHED] Terms: plans are priced annually, with a subsidised licence for up to 5 years. 100% ownership, no Emirati partner needed, and Dtec takes no equity. Open to all nationalities for tech-focused activities. You do not have to be in Dubai to apply.

## Membership types
- [PUBLISHED] Flexi (hot) desk: up to 2 residency visas. The member must use it at least 36 hours a month or the licence and lease can be cancelled.
- [PUBLISHED] Fixed desk: a landline and drawer storage, up to 3 visas.
- [PUBLISHED] Private or small office: 12 to 50 square metres, furnished or not. Visa allowance depends on the space.
- [PUBLISHED] Coworking only or short term: a day, a week, or up to 3 months, with no company set-up.
- [PUBLISHED] Dtec applicants need coloured passport, UAE visa and Emirates ID copies, CVs, the manager's attested degree and attested birth certificates. Some nationalities have extra requirements: ask a Dtec advisor.
- [NOT PUBLISHED] Public annual prices for desks and offices. Reported third-party figures vary widely: do not quote them. Reference figures in the DSO services handbook are marked as agency references.

## Meeting and event spaces
- [PUBLISHED] TechnoHub 1: Cafe Meeting Room (4 people), Majlis (6), Meeting Rooms 1 and 2, SAP Room (8), Du Executive Boardroom (14), Games Room and the Courtyard (up to 75 standing).
- [PUBLISHED] Dubai Digital Park: Auditorium (100), a Themed Meeting Room (6), Board Room on the third floor, Meeting Rooms 1 and 2, and the Map Room (14).
- [NOT PUBLISHED] Room prices. A small innovation fee of AED 20 applies to bookings over AED 50. Catering from the One Byte cafe: 04 371 2888.

## Programmes
- [PUBLISHED] Oraseya Capital is DIEZ's venture arm with a AED 500 million fund for seed to Series B. Dtec also runs the Sandbox programme, accelerators, MVP labs, the Dtec Forum and an annual pitch competition.

## Offices and desks run by DSO
- [PUBLISHED] Per the services handbook: Flexi Desk up to 2 visas, Fixed Desk up to 3 visas, Furnished Office (12 to 50 square metres) 2 visas per desk. Prices there are agency references, not DSO tariffs.
- [PUBLISHED] TechnoHub 4 and TechnoHub 5 are listed as DSO property offerings.
