## Location
- [REPORTED] DSO sits at the junction of Al Ain Road (E66) and Sheikh Mohammed bin Zayed Road (E311), in the Nad Hessa area of Dubai. The head office building is also nicknamed the Pineapple Building.
- [REPORTED] Dubai International Airport is about 20 kilometres away, roughly 17 to 19 minutes by car.

## Metro and buses
- [REPORTED] DSO has no Dubai Metro station yet. The nearest are Centrepoint on the Red Line and Dubai Healthcare City 2 on the Green Line, each roughly 15 minutes by car.
- [PUBLISHED] The Dubai Metro Blue Line will have a station in DSO. It is a 30-kilometre line with completion targeted for 2029.
- [PUBLISHED] RTA bus routes in effect from 17 July 2026: route 35 (International City to DSO, every 30 minutes at peak), routes 37A and 37B (International City and DSO Lake, every 30 minutes at peak).
- [REPORTED] Route 31 links DSO to Dubai Outsource City, and X25 runs between Al Karama bus station and DSO. Bus routes change: for a specific journey, point the caller to the RTA app.

- [PUBLISHED] Dubai Taxi runs 24 hours and can be booked on 800 88088.
- [REPORTED] Paid on-street parking in DSO is operated by Parkonic, with automatic billing through Salik. Rates and zones are not confirmed: do not state them.

## Resident services
- [PUBLISHED] Tenancy contracts are registered through Dubai Land Department (Ejari). DEWA move-in uses the Ejari or premise number.
- [PUBLISHED] DSO's HADI service in 2020 included maintenance requests and lease renewals for DSO-managed residential property. Today, maintenance and lease services are in the HALA services handbook.
