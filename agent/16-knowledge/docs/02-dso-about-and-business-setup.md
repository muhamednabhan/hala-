# Dubai Silicon Oasis: about, authority and business setup

How to use this document: every line carries a tag.
- [PUBLISHED] was read on an official DSO, DIEZ, UAE government or RTA page in November 2026. State it plainly.
- [REPORTED] comes from news or property sites and may be out of date. Say "reportedly" or "about", and offer a person for anything that matters.
- [NOT PUBLISHED] means the information could not be found. Say you do not know and offer a person.

## What DSO is
- [PUBLISHED] Dubai Silicon Oasis (DSO) is a free zone that combines business, residential and education. DSO describes itself as "a 15-minute city for business and sustainable living".
- [PUBLISHED] DSO was established by Law No. 16 of 2005. The site covers about 7 square kilometres.
- [PUBLISHED] DSO is part of the Dubai Integrated Economic Zones Authority (DIEZ, also written DIEZA), established in 2021. DIEZ brings together Dubai Airport Free Zone, Dubai Silicon Oasis and Dubai CommerCity. The former authority name was Dubai Silicon Oasis Authority (DSOA).
- [PUBLISHED] DIEZ has more than 5,000 registered companies across 20 industries, counting all three zones. The Chairman is HH Sheikh Ahmed bin Saeed Al Maktoum and the Executive Chairman is HE Dr Mohammed Alzarooni.
- [PUBLISHED] DSO's homepage lists 11 industry clusters, 60,000+ talents, and 15+ learning and R&D institutions. It was ranked a global zone for SMEs and R&D by fDi Insights, and named Global Free Zone of the Year for SMEs in October 2023.
- [PUBLISHED] DSO is a qualified free zone under UAE Corporate Tax Law, with a 0% rate on qualifying income.
- [REPORTED] Company and resident counts vary across pages (for example 28,000 to 40,000 companies and 90,000 to 100,000 residents). Say "tens of thousands of companies and around a hundred thousand residents", and do not quote an exact figure.
- [PUBLISHED] Named in DSO's own material: Rochester Institute of Technology (RIT) Dubai, Dtec, Dubai Digital Park, TechnoHub 4 and TechnoHub 5. Companies named include Porsche, Jaguar Land Rover, Hyundai, Mitsubishi, W Motors, Henkel, Orange and Wacker.
- [REPORTED] RIT Dubai opened in 2008 and is across the road from Dubai Academic City. Phone 04 371 2000.
- [REPORTED] A 24-hour walk-in Smart Police Station opened in DSO in 2020.
- [REPORTED] Dubai Digital Park is about 150,000 square metres, with offices, retail, apartments and a convention centre.

## Business entities and licences
- [PUBLISHED] Entity types: Free Zone Company (FZCO) for new businesses, and Branch for an existing company. A branch can only do the parent company's activities.
- [PUBLISHED] Single-shareholder FZE is no longer used; since the 2023 regulations an existing FZE is treated as an FZCO.
- [PUBLISHED] Operating licences: Service (specified services), Trade (import, export, distribution of goods) and Industrial (import raw materials, manufacture, process, assemble, package, export).
- [PUBLISHED] A Business Operation Permit is for companies in the Administrative Zone, the areas run by private developers. It is based on a Dubai Department of Economy and Tourism licence and needs no share capital.
- [PUBLISHED] Transfer from another free zone uses a Certificate of Continuation and a Certificate of Cancellation.
- [PUBLISHED] Activities are chosen from the ISIC and DED activity lists. There is no separately named e-commerce or freelancer licence on dso.ae: ask an advisor.

## How to set up (official steps)
- [PUBLISHED] Stage 1: a personal consultation with a DSO advisor. Stage 2: documents and approval, then residency and business banking (the advisor can introduce partner banks). Stage 3: community integration through clusters, events and the client portal.
- [PUBLISHED] On the portal: submit the application on eservices.dso.ae, pay fees and submit the legal documents, sign the e-agreement and share-capital letter, pass the document pre-approval including an activity and security check, receive the e-agreement, then the e-licence.
- [PUBLISHED] General document checklist: Letter of Intent, passport copies, an NOC for UAE residents, a bank statement or reference letter or audited financials, the application form and a business plan.
- [PUBLISHED] For FZCO or Branch initial approval: the service licence request, a Power of Attorney if someone signs for the founders, a Project Summary Letter, passports of shareholders and manager, the MOA and certificate of incorporation for a branch or corporate shareholder, the parent's trade licence for local companies, the Unit Title Deed and the Office Tenancy Contract before registration.
- [PUBLISHED] Business Operation Permit documents: manager passport, the DED trade licence, the Unit Title Deed and the Office Tenancy Contract.
- [PUBLISHED] Minimum start-up capital for an FZE or FZCO is AED 100,000. FZCO shares are in multiples of AED 10,000.
- [PUBLISHED] Published timing: the application takes about 7 days, and the licence and lease agreement are issued within 2 days of receiving all legal documents and payment. Registration time depends on the applicant's documents.
- [NOT PUBLISHED] Official fee amounts for licences, visas and the Establishment Card, and a general visa quota rule. Say fees are given by an advisor or the portal. Visa allowances for desks are in the Dtec document.
- [REPORTED] Third-party estimates for a first year are AED 20,000 to 40,000 all in. These are not DSO figures: do not quote them.
- [PUBLISHED] DSO's Government Services department handles employment visas and work permits. The HADI announcement of 2020 lists the company establishment card, employment package amendment and cancellation, and bank guarantees as e-services.
