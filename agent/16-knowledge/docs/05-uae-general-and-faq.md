# UAE general facts a front desk gets asked

Tags: [PUBLISHED] official page, say plainly. [REPORTED] secondary source, say "reportedly". [NOT PUBLISHED] say you do not know and offer a person. Never quote government fees: they change and are not held here.

## Week and holidays
- [PUBLISHED] The UAE weekend is Saturday and Sunday. Dubai and federal government offices work 7:30 AM to 3:30 PM Monday to Thursday and 7:30 AM to 12 noon on Friday. DSO's own customer-service hours are different (8 AM to 3 PM, Monday to Friday): always quote DSO's.
- [PUBLISHED] Public holidays: New Year's Day (1 January), Eid Al Fitr (1 to 3 Shawwal), Arafah Day (9 Dhu Al Hijjah), Eid Al Adha (10 to 12 Dhu Al Hijjah), Hijri New Year (1 Muharram), the Prophet's Birthday (12 Rabi Al Awwal), and National Day (2 and 3 December). Islamic dates depend on moon sighting, so say the exact days are announced shortly before.
- [PUBLISHED] National Day is 2 and 3 December every year.

## Emirates ID and visas (general, not DSO fees)
- [PUBLISHED] The Federal Authority for Identity, Citizenship, Customs and Port Security (ICP) issues Emirates IDs and residence visas. A residence visa and the Emirates ID expire together: when the visa ends, the ID ends.
- [PUBLISHED] An Emirates ID can be renewed within 6 months before it expires and there is a grace period after.
- [NOT PUBLISHED] Current government fees: do not quote any.

## Handy
- [PUBLISHED] Dubai Taxi: 800 88088 (24 hours). Emergency: police 999, ambulance 998, fire 997.
- [PUBLISHED] Salik is Dubai's road toll and Nol is the public-transport card.
- Greetings: "Ahlan wa sahlan" welcome; "Marhaba" hello; "Shukran" thank you; "Afwan" you're welcome.

## Common questions and safe answers
- "Is DSO open today?" DSO customer service is open Monday to Friday, 8 AM to 3 PM Dubai time. Weekends and public holidays are closed. (Say you cannot see today's date unless the tool gives it.)
- "Can I get a licence the same day?" The published process says about 7 days for the application and 2 days to issue once all documents and payment are in. Offer a business-setup advisor on the phone line.
- "How much does it cost to set up?" Official fees are not published online. Offer an advisor. Never quote third-party estimates.
- "Where do I pay?" Fees are paid with the DSO e-wallet on the portal, or at the 24-hour deposit machine in the DSO headquarters lobby.
- "Can I talk to a person?" Yes: hand over straight away.
