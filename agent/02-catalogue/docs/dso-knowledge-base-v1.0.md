## 1. Trade Licence Types, Activities & Amendments (Corporate)

### 1.1 DSO Licence Categories & Permitted Activities
Companies in DSO operate primarily as a **Free Zone Company (FZCO)** (under 2023 DIEZA regulations, former single-shareholder FZEs are also classified as FZCOs) or a **Branch** of a UAE/foreign parent company.
- **1. Service Licence (Most Common for Tech & Dtec):**
  - *Permitted Activities:* Software development, SaaS, IT consultancy, AI & robotics R&D, cybersecurity, digital marketing, management consultancy, and professional/creative services.
  - *Premises Fit:* Eligible for Dtec Flexi Desk (up to 2 visas), Fixed Desk (up to 3 visas), or Furnished Office (2 visas per desk).
- **2. Trade Licence (Commercial & E-Commerce):**
  - *Permitted Activities:* Import, export, storage, and distribution of specified goods (e.g., IT hardware, electronics, smart devices, e-commerce trading, and general trading subject to screening).
  - *Premises Fit:* Office space or DSO warehouse for stock-holding traders.
- **3. Industrial Licence:**
  - *Permitted Activities:* Importing raw materials, light manufacturing, electronics/PCB assembly, 3D printing at production scale, packaging, and exporting finished products.
  - *Premises Fit:* DSO Light Industrial Units (LIUs).
- **4. Business Operation Permit (Special Permit):**
  - *Permitted Activities:* Allows companies holding a Dubai Department of Economy and Tourism (DET / mainland) licence to operate within privately developed freehold/leasehold areas of DSO's Administrative Zone (such as retail outlets, cafes, clinics, and salons).
- **Activities Requiring External Authority Pre-Approval:** Media, educational training, healthcare/clinics, telecom equipment, virtual assets, food/cosmetics, and regulated financial/DNFBP activities (which also require goAML registration).
- **Activity Grouping Rule:** Multiple activities can sit under one licence if they belong to the same segment/category; adding activities across different segments (e.g., Service + Trade) incurs an additional segment fee (**AED 3,000 to AED 5,000/year [DEMO DATA]**).

### 1.2 Trade Licence Renewal
- **Voice Summary:** "To renew your DSO trade licence, you first renew your office or desk lease on eservices.dso.ae, confirm your UBO details, and settle the renewal voucher via your DSO e-wallet."
- **Required Documents:**
  1. Valid renewed DSO Lease / Flexi-Desk agreement
  2. Updated Ultimate Beneficial Owner (UBO) declaration confirmation
  3. Audited financial report (if applicable to the entity)
- **Process Steps:**
  1. Log into https://eservices.dso.ae/ and complete lease renewal.
  2. Submit the Licence Renewal request and generate the payment voucher.
  3. Pay via DSO e-wallet (or the 24-hour deposit kiosk at DSO HQ).
  4. Download the renewed digital E-Licence.
- **Estimated Timeline:** 1 to 2 working days **[DEMO DATA]**
- **Estimated Fee:** AED 12,000 licence renewal + workspace lease fee **[DEMO DATA]**
- **Dispute / Edge-Case Policy:** If a company has late-renewal penalties or disputes the renewal invoice amount, do not negotiate—log the enquiry and transfer to a DSO Licensing Officer (600 59 55 57).

### 1.3 Trade Licence Amendment (Activity, Name, Manager, or Shareholding)
- **Voice Summary:** "You can amend your business activities, company name, manager, or shareholders through the DSO portal by submitting a board resolution and supporting IDs."
- **Required Documents:**
  1. Notarized/signed Board Resolution approving the specific amendment
  2. Current Trade Licence and Memorandum of Association (MOA)
  3. Passport and Emirates ID copies of new manager/shareholders (if changing management/ownership)
  4. External regulator NOC (if adding a regulated activity)
- **Process Steps:**
  1. Apply under "Licence Amendment" on https://eservices.dso.ae/.
  2. Complete DSO legal and security clearance.
  3. E-sign the MOA Addendum and pay amendment fees.
  4. Receive the amended E-Licence.
- **Estimated Timeline:** 3 to 5 working days **[DEMO DATA]**

## 2. Employment Visa Services (Corporate HR & PRO)

### 2.1 New Employment Visa
- **Voice Summary:** "For a new employment visa, we first apply for an entry permit on the DSO portal, followed by medical fitness testing, Emirates ID biometrics, and residency issuance."
- **Eligibility:** Active DSO company with valid Establishment Card and available visa quota (Flexi Desk: 2 visas; Fixed Desk: 3 visas; Furnished Office: 2 per desk).
- **Required Documents:**
  1. Employee passport copy (minimum 6 months validity)
  2. High-resolution white-background passport photo
  3. Attested university/educational degree (required for managerial/skilled roles)
  4. Signed DSO standard Employment Contract
  5. Previous UAE visa cancellation paper or tourist entry stamp (if inside the UAE)
- **Process Steps:**
  1. Submit Employment Entry Permit on https://eservices.dso.ae/.
  2. Complete in-country status change (or airport entry if abroad).
  3. Complete UAE Medical Fitness Test and Emirates ID biometrics.
  4. Upload medical & EID receipt on the portal for digital residency issuance.
- **Estimated Timeline:** 5 to 7 working days **[DEMO DATA]**
- **Estimated Fee:** AED 3,500 (Standard) / AED 4,800 (Urgent) **[DEMO DATA]**

### 2.2 Employment Visa Renewal
- **Voice Summary:** "To renew an employee's visa, you'll need their passport copy, current visa and Emirates ID, a fresh medical fitness certificate, and active health insurance."
- **Required Documents:**
  1. Passport copy (valid for at least 6 months)
  2. Current UAE Residence Visa and Emirates ID copies
  3. Recent passport photo (white background)
  4. Passed Medical Fitness Test result
  5. Active UAE Health Insurance certificate
- **Process Steps:**
  1. Select "Employment Visa Renewal" on https://eservices.dso.ae/.
  2. Upload updated medical, insurance, and passport documents.
  3. Pay renewal fee via DSO e-wallet.
  4. Receive renewed digital residency and updated Emirates ID.
- **Estimated Timeline:** 3 to 5 working days **[DEMO DATA]**
- **Estimated Fee:** AED 2,800 **[DEMO DATA]**

### 2.3 Employment Visa Cancellation
- **Voice Summary:** "To cancel an employment visa, upload the signed cancellation form, employee passport and Emirates ID copies, and end-of-service settlement confirmation on the portal."
- **Required Documents:**
  1. DSO Visa Cancellation Form signed by both the employee and authorized company signatory
  2. Employee passport and Emirates ID copies
  3. End-of-service gratuity/settlement acknowledgment
- **Process Steps:**
  1. Submit cancellation request on https://eservices.dso.ae/.
  2. Clear any pending immigration fines or e-wallet dues.
  3. Download the official Visa Cancellation Certificate (the employee receives a grace period to exit or transfer to a new sponsor).
