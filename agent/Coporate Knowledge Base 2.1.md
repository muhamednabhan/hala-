## 1. Trade Licence Types, Activities & Amendments (Corporate)

### 1.1 DSO Licence Categories & Permitted Activities
Companies in DSO operate primarily as a **Free Zone Company (FZCO)** (under 2023 DIEZA regulations, former single-shareholder FZEs are also classified as FZCOs) or a **Branch** of a UAE/foreign parent company[span_83](start_span)[span_83](end_span).
- **1. Service Licence (Most Common for Tech & Dtec):**
  - *Permitted Activities:* Software development, SaaS, IT consultancy, AI & robotics R&D, cybersecurity, digital marketing, management consultancy, and professional/creative services[span_84](start_span)[span_84](end_span).
  - *Premises Fit:* Eligible for Dtec Flexi Desk (up to 2 visas), Fixed Desk (up to 3 visas), or Furnished Office (2 visas per desk)[span_85](start_span)[span_85](end_span).
- **2. Trade Licence (Commercial & E-Commerce):**
  - *Permitted Activities:* Import, export, storage, and distribution of specified goods (e.g., IT hardware, electronics, smart devices, e-commerce trading, and general trading subject to screening)[span_86](start_span)[span_86](end_span).
  - *Premises Fit:* Office space or DSO warehouse for stock-holding traders[span_87](start_span)[span_87](end_span).
- **3. Industrial Licence:**
  - *Permitted Activities:* Importing raw materials, light manufacturing, electronics/PCB assembly, 3D printing at production scale, packaging, and exporting finished products[span_88](start_span)[span_88](end_span).
  - *Premises Fit:* DSO Light Industrial Units (LIUs)[span_89](start_span)[span_89](end_span).
- **4. Business Operation Permit (Special Permit):**
  - *Permitted Activities:* Allows companies holding a Dubai Department of Economy and Tourism (DET / mainland) licence to operate within privately developed freehold/leasehold areas of DSO's Administrative Zone (such as retail outlets, cafes, clinics, and salons)[span_90](start_span)[span_90](end_span).
- **Activities Requiring External Authority Pre-Approval:** Media, educational training, healthcare/clinics, telecom equipment, virtual assets, food/cosmetics, and regulated financial/DNFBP activities (which also require goAML registration)[span_91](start_span)[span_91](end_span).
- **Activity Grouping Rule:** Multiple activities can sit under one licence if they belong to the same segment/category; adding activities across different segments (e.g., Service + Trade) incurs an additional segment fee (**AED 3,000 to AED 5,000/year [DEMO DATA]**)[span_92](start_span)[span_92](end_span).
